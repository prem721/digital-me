Digital Me — AI-Powered Digital Twin

Digital Me is an AI-powered digital representation of myself that aims to allow people to interact with a virtual version of me. The purpose is to create a system that understands my personal background, education, technical skills, experiences, interests, communication style, and preferences so that people can learn about me through natural conversation. The project has two main modes: Presentation Mode and Companion Mode.

Presentation Mode is designed for people who want to know more about me without needing to speak with me directly. They can ask questions about my skills, education, projects, experiences, hobbies, and goals, and the system generates responses using information stored in my personal knowledge base. Retrieval-augmented generation helps the AI retrieve relevant information and produce answers grounded in my actual background.

Companion Mode is designed to provide a more personal conversational experience. Instead of behaving like a generic chatbot, it aims to communicate in my style and use relevant conversation history, preferences, and approved memories to provide contextual responses. The goal is to create an AI that feels like talking to a digital version of myself during everyday conversations, brainstorming sessions, or moments when I need encouragement or suggestions.

The planned architecture includes a large language model, RAG, a structured personal database, vector search, conversational memory, personality modelling, backend APIs, and a frontend interface. Additional planned features include voice cloning, speech recognition, facial-expression analysis, avatar animation, and lip synchronization. Privacy and security are important aspects of the project. The system should distinguish public information from private information, prevent unauthorized access, protect personal memories, and require my approval before permanent changes are made to my digital identity. The overall goal is to combine generative AI, personalization, multimodal interaction, and security into a single digital identity platform.

MarketAI Suite — Generative AI-Powered Sales and Marketing Intelligence

MarketAI Suite is an AI-powered sales and marketing intelligence project designed to support marketing campaign creation, lead evaluation, sales communication, and performance analysis. The idea is to use generative AI and intelligent analysis to help users improve their sales and marketing workflows. Its planned features include AI-powered campaign generation, lead scoring, a conversational interface, real-time sales pitch refinement, campaign performance analytics, predictive collaboration planning, and marketing poster generation.

The project involves technologies such as Python, Flask, JavaScript, and AI service integration through Groq. The backend exposes API endpoints that allow the frontend to request AI-generated campaign content and lead-scoring results. The project also involves integrating frontend interactions with backend services and debugging issues related to API calls, JavaScript functions, and application functionality. The objective is to develop an intelligent platform that combines generative AI with sales and marketing workflows. The implementation status of individual features should be verified before presenting them as completed capabilities.

KrishiRakshak AI / PestSense IoT — AI-Powered Precision Agriculture

KrishiRakshak AI, also referred to as PestSense IoT, is an agricultural technology project focused on identifying potential pest risks and supporting smarter crop-protection decisions. The concept combines artificial intelligence, computer vision, IoT sensors, and predictive analytics to help farmers monitor agricultural conditions and respond to potential pest problems earlier.

The proposed system uses RGB or multispectral cameras, pheromone-trap monitoring, and environmental sensors that measure parameters such as temperature, humidity, and soil moisture. AI models can analyse image data and environmental observations to estimate pest risks. The proposed platform also includes cloud-based monitoring, geographical risk mapping, mobile notifications, regional-language alerts, and recommendations based on integrated pest management practices.

The planned technology stack includes computer vision, lightweight convolutional neural networks, time-series analysis, IoT connectivity, cloud services, and geospatial data management. LoRaWAN and cellular connectivity are potential communication options for field deployments. Solar-powered operation is another proposed feature for suitable agricultural environments. The overall goal is to explore how AI and IoT can support early pest-risk identification, more informed agricultural decisions, and sustainable farming practices. Predictive alerts and field-deployment capabilities should be described as planned features unless their effectiveness has been demonstrated through implementation and testing.

Smart Skill-to-Career Mapping and Opportunity Recommendation Platform

The Smart Skill-to-Career Mapping and Opportunity Recommendation Platform is a project concept intended to help engineering students identify suitable career paths based on their existing skills, interests, learning progress, and professional goals. The system aims to connect students with relevant career opportunities and identify the skills they need to develop to become suitable candidates for particular roles.

Proposed features include student skill profiling, career recommendations, skill-gap analysis, learning-resource recommendations, and opportunity matching. AI techniques such as natural language processing, semantic similarity, recommendation systems, and ranking algorithms could help match student profiles with relevant opportunities. Technologies explored for this project include React, Node.js, Express, database integration, and AI-powered recommendation components. The overall objective is to make career planning more personalised and help students make informed decisions about their technical development.
