Goals

My main goal is to become a skilled software engineer specializing in artificial intelligence and intelligent application development. I want to understand how to build complete applications, from designing the architecture and developing APIs to integrating databases, implementing AI models, securing the application, and deploying it. I am particularly interested in generative AI, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, machine learning, computer vision, and AI agents. I want to build projects that go beyond basic API integration and demonstrate a deeper understanding of how different technologies work together. I also want to improve my problem-solving abilities, participate in hackathons, work on innovative ideas, and gain practical experience developing complex projects. In the long term, I want to turn my technical knowledge and creativity into useful products that solve meaningful problems.
